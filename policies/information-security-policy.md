## Purpose

Harrowgate Digital keeps customer documents and staff data accurate, available to the right people, and out of everyone else's hands. This policy is the one the Managing Director signs. The other policies say how.

## Who it covers

Everyone who works for Harrowgate, including contractors with access to company systems. It covers the scope in HG-ISMS-001.

## Rules

1. Customer content is Restricted. It is handled only inside Client Ledger, company email, and the support desk.
2. Access is granted because a role needs it, and it is removed when the role ends.
3. Company laptops are the only devices used for customer content. They stay encrypted.
4. A lost laptop, a wrong-recipient email, or a suspicion that an account is being misused is reported to the ISMS Manager the same day.
5. Suppliers do not receive customer content or staff data until they are on the asset register and the contract covers confidentiality and breach notice.
6. Exceptions are written down, time-limited, and approved by the Managing Director.

## Responsibilities

The Managing Director approves this policy and the residual risks. The ISMS Manager runs the management system and reports quarterly. Engineering protects the product. People runs joiners, movers, and leavers. Finance owns supplier contracts and the office. Every person follows the policy and reports events.
