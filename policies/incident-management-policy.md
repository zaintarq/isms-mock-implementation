# Incident management policy

| Date | 23 October 2026 |

## Purpose

Make sure a security event is reported, judged, and closed, including when the Manchester office is shut.

## What to report

A lost or stolen laptop, a mailbox that looks misused, a customer file in the wrong place, a production change nobody recognises, malware alert, or a supplier saying they have had a breach. Staff tell the ISMS Manager the same day. If the ISMS Manager is unavailable, they tell the Engineering Lead.

## Severity

| Level | Meaning | Who leads |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Low | One device or one account, no sign that customer content was read | ISMS Manager |
| Medium | Customer content may have been exposed to the wrong staff member or a single customer | ISMS Manager, MD informed the same day |
| High | Customer content may have left the company, or production is down with no clear restore | Managing Director |

## First actions

1. Write down what is known, including the time.
2. Disable the affected account or remote-wipe the laptop if it is missing.
3. Leave logs in place. Export a copy into a restricted incident folder. Do not edit the export.
4. The ISMS Manager decides whether this is an incident. The Managing Director decides whether a customer or the ICO is told.
5. When it is over, write one page: what happened, what was done, and whether the risk register changes.

Harrowgate does not run a 24-hour desk. High severity still gets a same-day call to the Managing Director, including at the weekend, using the contact list stored in the password manager.
