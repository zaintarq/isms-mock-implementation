## Purpose

Say what staff may do with company systems and customer content. This is the policy people acknowledge at joining and once a year.

## Allowed

- Company email, the file suite, Client Ledger, and the support desk for customer work.
- Reasonable personal use of the laptop that does not install software, does not weaken the disk encryption, and does not store customer content in a personal account.

## Not allowed

- Sending customer content to a personal mailbox, a personal cloud drive, or an unapproved tool.
- Printing customer content, unless the Engineering Lead agrees for a named document and the paper is shredded the same day.
- Turning off disk encryption, endpoint protection, or the screen lock.
- Plugging in USB storage. It is blocked on the image.
- Letting someone else use your laptop session or your MFA prompt.
- Storing card numbers. Harrowgate does not take them. A customer who emails one is asked to use the hosted checkout, and the mail is deleted.

## Remote work

Work from home is normal. The company laptop is used. The screen is locked when you step away. The laptop is not left in a car overnight.

## If something goes wrong

Report it the same day under the incident policy. A mistake reported promptly is handled as an incident. Hiding it is a conduct matter under the staff handbook.
