# Supplier security policy

| Company | Harrowgate Digital Ltd (fictional) |
| Version | 1.0 |
| Owner | Finance Lead |
| Date | 23 October 2026 |

## Purpose

Know which suppliers can see customer content or staff data, and put that in the contract before they do.

## Before a supplier starts

Finance does not sign, and Engineering does not connect, a supplier who will process Restricted or Confidential data until:

- the supplier is a row on the asset register, with an owner
- the contract includes confidentiality, a security contact, and breach notice without undue delay
- the data location is the UK, or the Managing Director has accepted another location in writing

The cloud, email, payroll, and support-desk suppliers are the current high group. The payment checkout is in scope only for the payment-status feed. Card numbers stay with that supplier.

## Review

Once a year the ISMS Manager and the asset owner check the four high suppliers: is the contract still right, did they notify any incident, does access still match the need. Smaller tools are not on this cycle yet. That gap is control A.5.22, status Partial, and it is risk R-04.

## When a supplier reports a breach

Treat it as an incident under HG-POL-004. The supplier's notice does not replace Harrowgate's decision about telling customers.
